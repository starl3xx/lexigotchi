# Lexigotchi — Product Spec v0.1

**Status:** Draft for review · **Owner:** starl3xx · **Date:** June 10, 2026
**Ecosystem:** $WORD / Let's Have A Word! (Base) · **Surfaces:** Farcaster mini app + standalone web

> **One-liner:** Raise your letters. Spell your words. Own the dictionary.
>
> Lexigotchi is a tamagotchi-style collection game where players mint animated rubber-hose letter characters, gamble $WORD on chance-based upgrades from lowercase to UPPERCASE, assemble letters into permanent word claims from the LHAW canonical dictionary, and stake those words to earn $WORD — with a daily jackpot that fires whenever a staked word turns out to be the day's Let's Have A Word! answer.

---

## 1. Problem statement & strategic fit

$WORD currently has one utility surface: the daily LHAW skill game. That gives it a sharp but narrow demand profile — engagement is concentrated in a single daily session, and there is no reason to *hold* $WORD between sessions beyond speculation. Players who churn from the daily game exit the ecosystem entirely.

Lexigotchi adds a second, structurally different surface: a collection game with persistent assets, daily care loops, and yield mechanics — all denominated in $WORD. It converts $WORD from "entry fee token" into "economy token," creates holding demand (you need a balance to mint, roll, claim, and feed), and cross-pollinates with LHAW in both directions: the daily LHAW answer doubles as Lexigotchi's lottery draw, and Lexigotchi assets give LHAW's reveal moment a second audience. Critically, the game is designed as a **closed-loop economy**: yield paid to stakers is funded by fees paid by collectors and gamblers, not by token emissions.

## 2. Goals

1. **Create durable $WORD utility:** ≥ 30% of circulating $WORD activity (by tx count) flows through Lexigotchi contracts within 90 days of full launch.
2. **Grow the collector base:** 1,000+ unique wallets holding at least one Letter within 30 days of mint; ≥ 10% of the dictionary (~440 words) claimed within 60 days of claims opening.
3. **Build a daily retention loop:** ≥ 35% D7 retention among wallets with at least one staked word (the feed-your-word loop).
4. **Cross-pollinate the ecosystem:** ≥ 25% of active LHAW players hold at least one Lexigotchi asset within 90 days, and the daily jackpot reveal becomes a recurring social moment on Farcaster.
5. **Stay solvent by construction:** reward liabilities remain bounded and fully fee-funded at every point — no emissions schedule, no IOU APR.

## 3. Non-goals

- **No open dictionary.** Only the 4,438-word LHAW canonical list is claimable. No user-submitted words, no 4- or 6-letter expansion in v1. The bounded set *is* the product.
- **No letter death or downgrade.** Neglect pauses yield and saddens the animation; it never burns or reverts assets. (Loot-box optics, player trust, and support load all point the same way.)
- **No in-game fiat or ETH pricing.** Everything is denominated in $WORD. ETH touches the system only at the DEX.
- **No PvP/battle mechanics in v1.** The competitive surface is collection, completion, and the jackpot — not combat. (Parking lot for seasons.)
- **No bespoke marketplace at launch.** Letters and Words are standard tokens; existing Base marketplaces handle secondary. We integrate, not build.

## 4. Core concepts

| Concept | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Letter** | ERC-1155 token. 52 token IDs: `a–z` (lowercase) and `A–Z` (uppercase). Fungible within an ID. The tamagotchi characters. |
| **Word** | ERC-721 NFT, one per dictionary word (tokenId = keccak256 of the word). Minted by escrowing its 5 Letters + a claim fee. Globally unique, permanent until voluntarily dissolved. |
| **Case state** | A Word is `lowercase` (all 5 letters lowercase), `UPPERCASE` (all 5 upper), or `Mixed` (in transition). Case determines yield multiplier and art. |
| **Stake** | Earn-mode toggle on a Word you own. Staked Words accrue $WORD from the Rewards Pool and are eligible for the daily Jackpot — but they get hungry. |
| **The Lexidex** | The album: all 4,438 words, their rarity tier, claim status, case state, and trophy history. Personal and global completion tracking. |
| **Jackpot** | Daily pot paid to the staked owner of the day's LHAW answer. Rolls over when the answer is unclaimed or unstaked. |
| **Trophy** | Permanent gold-star variant marked on any word that has been an LHAW answer. Lottery value spent; collection value up. Trophies attach to the word in the global registry and survive dissolution/re-claim. |

## 5. Game design

### 5.1 Player loops

**Session loop (minutes):** open app → check letters/words (animation states communicate everything) → feed hungry words → maybe roll an upgrade → maybe browse the Lexidex for a claimable target.

**Daily loop:** LHAW answer reveals → jackpot fires or rolls over → broadcast cast ("Today's word was CRANE — the CRANE holder just won X $WORD") → feed staked words → claim daily streak snack (small free feed for showing up).

**Long arc (weeks–months):** complete words → chase tier completion in the Lexidex → hunt crowns (the 45 Legendaries) → upgrade claimed words to full UPPERCASE → trade on secondary → flex trophies.

### 5.2 Letter minting

- Letters mint in **packs of 5** ("racks" — the inventory UI is a Scrabble rack), priced in $WORD. Single pulls available at a slight premium per letter.
- **Pull odds mirror dictionary demand** (see Appendix A): each letter's mint probability equals its share of the dictionary's 22,190 letter slots. S ≈ 10.6%, E ≈ 10.4% … Q ≈ 0.20%. This makes odds fair, legible, and publishable as a provable table.
- All mints are **lowercase**. Uppercase exists only through upgrade rolls.
- **Global supply cap: 55,475 letters (2.5× total dictionary demand).** Full-dictionary completion is mathematically possible; hoarding makes rare letters genuinely contested. *(Tunable before deploy; 2.0×–3.0× is the sane band.)*
- Randomness via the same commit-reveal pattern as LHAW/EGGS: commit to a pack purchase, reveal assigns letters.

### 5.3 Upgrade rolls (lowercase → UPPERCASE)

- Roll on any lowercase Letter you own — including Letters escrowed inside a claimed Word (this keeps the core sink alive after claiming; a Word with some upgraded letters shows as `Mixed`).
- **Two-step commit-reveal** (commit fee + roll, reveal resolves), identical architecture to EGGS' "Commit To Level Up → Level Up Chicken."
- **Base success: 25%. Pity: +5 percentage points per consecutive failure on that specific token, capped at 60%, reset on success.** Worst case ≈ 8 rolls; expected ≈ 3.2. *(Tunable.)*
- **On failure, nothing is lost but the fee.** The Letter never burns, never downgrades, never scrambles. This is a hard design and compliance commitment.
- On success: the lowercase 1155 burns, the uppercase ID mints to the owner (or into the Word's escrow). Trigger the share moment (§7).
- Roll fee is flat in v1 regardless of letter rarity. *(P2: rarity-scaled roll fees.)*

### 5.4 Claiming words

- Own all 5 Letters of an eligible word (any case, but uniform — all lower or all upper; Mixed sets cannot initiate a claim) → pay the claim fee → the 5 Letters escrow into a newly minted Word NFT.
- **Claims are unique and permanent.** First to claim MONEY owns MONEY until they choose otherwise.
- **Dissolution:** a Word owner may burn the Word NFT to recover its 5 escrowed Letters. The name returns to the claimable pool (re-claim drama is a feature). Trophy history persists on the word in the registry.
- Claim fee is flat across tiers in v1 — the letter market already prices rarity. *(Tunable.)*

### 5.5 Staking & yield

- Staking toggles earn-mode on a claimed Word. Unstaking is instant and free; the claim is never at risk.
- The **Rewards Pool** pays out daily, pro-rata by **stake weight = tier weight × case multiplier**:

| Tier | Weight | | Case | Multiplier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 1 | | lowercase | 1× |
| Uncommon | 2 | | Mixed | 1× (lowercase rate) |
| Rare | 3 | | UPPERCASE | 2× |
| Epic | 5 | | | |
| Legendary | 8 | | | |

- Daily distribution = a fixed % of the current Rewards Pool balance (e.g., 1%/day), so payouts scale with real fee inflow and the pool can never be drained below solvency. **APR is emergent, never promised.**

### 5.6 Care (the tamagotchi layer)

- **Only staked Words get hungry.** Pure collectors are never punished; care is the price of yield.
- Each staked Word consumes one **Snack** per day. Snacks are bought with $WORD and **100% of snack spend is burned** — your word literally eats $WORD.
- Hunger states are expressed *entirely through animation*, no health bars: fed words bounce and grin; one missed day = **Peckish** (arms droop, eyes half-lidded, yield −50%); three missed days = **Hungry** (full slump, snoring, yield paused). Feeding restores instantly and triggers the happy-dance.
- Push notification copy writes itself: *"your q is hungry."*
- Daily check-in grants one free Snack (streak mechanic, retention hook, lowers cost-to-play for small holders).

### 5.7 The daily jackpot (LHAW crossover)

- Every day, the LHAW answer is revealed. If that word is **claimed and staked**, its owner wins the entire Jackpot pool. If not, the pot **rolls over** — early escalating jackpots are the marketing engine for the claim rush.
- The word receives its permanent **Trophy** variant either way.
- **Hard dependency — provably fair answers:** once money rides on the draw, LHAW answer selection must be beyond suspicion, including from the team. LHAW commits a **hash chain of future answers on-chain before claims open**; each day's reveal is verified against the chain. No party (including us) can steer the draw toward a held word. This is P0 for Phase 3 and requires an LHAW contract/process change.
- With 4,438 words and no answer repeats, the draw runs ~12 years; every staked word is a lottery ticket with an unknown maturity date.

## 6. Tokenomics — $WORD flows

**Sinks (in):** pack mints, upgrade roll fees, claim fees, snacks (burned).
**Faucets (out):** staking yield, daily jackpot. Both are paid exclusively from the splits below — zero emissions.

| Revenue source | → Rewards Pool | → Jackpot | → Burn | → Treasury |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pack mints | 40% | 10% | 20% | 30% |
| Upgrade rolls | 40% | 25% | 20% | 15% |
| Claim fees | 25% | 25% | 25% | 25% |
| Snacks | — | — | 100% | — |

*(All splits tunable pre-deploy; the invariant is that Pool + Jackpot inflows ≥ outflow commitments at all times.)*

**Solvency by construction:** max stake weight is bounded (4,438 words × max multiplier), daily payout is a % of actual pool balance, and the jackpot only pays what it holds. There is no scenario where the game owes more than it has.

## 7. Art direction & production

**Style:** 1930s rubber hose — noodle limbs, white gloves, pie-cut eyes, squash-and-stretch. Lowercase letters are the kids (smaller, rounder, gap-toothed); UPPERCASE are the glow-ups (taller, top hats, monocles, spats). An upgrade should be visually legible from across the room.

**Character production:** 52 characters = 26 designs × 2 maturity variants on a **shared rig** with per-letter silhouettes and accessories. Animation states map 1:1 to game states: `idle`, `peckish`, `hungry`, `eating`, `rolling` (drumroll shiver), `upgrade-pop`, `celebrate`.

**A claimed Word is a chorus line:** five characters holding hands. Lowercase words shuffle side to side; full-UPPERCASE words do the synchronized kick. Trophy words wear medals. The day's jackpot winner does a confetti number.

**Pipeline, phased:**
1. **Phase 1:** static SVG characters + CSS keyframe idle/droop. Cheap, shippable, on-brand.
2. **Phase 3:** Rive state machines (purpose-built for exactly this — one .riv per character family, states driven by contract data via the indexer).
3. **Share moments:** Remotion renders a clip on every upgrade success and jackpot win (the starl3xx-sting pipeline, productized) — auto-attached to the compose-cast intent. This is the viral surface.

**Launch hero asset:** the JAZZY quintet — the game's rarest word — as a rubber hose jazz band. Poster, sting, and the eventual owner's permanent flex.

## 8. Architecture

**Contracts (Base):**
- `Letters.sol` — ERC-1155, 52 IDs, supply caps per ID, commit-reveal pack minting.
- `Words.sol` — ERC-721, claim/dissolve, letter escrow, case state, trophy registry (word-level, survives dissolution).
- `Rolls.sol` — commit-reveal upgrades, pity counters per token, fee routing.
- `Staking.sol` — stake toggles, weights, daily pro-rata distribution, hunger/snack accounting.
- `Jackpot.sol` — pot accrual, rollover, payout gated on answer verification.
- `AnswerChain` (LHAW-side) — on-chain hash-chain commitment of future answers; daily reveal + verify.
- Upgradeable proxies (the EGGS pattern), multisig admin, splits in storage (tunable without redeploys).

**App:** single Next.js codebase → Farcaster mini app (mini app SDK, Quick Auth, notifications, compose-cast intents) + standalone web (wagmi + Privy embedded wallets for non-crypto-native onboarding). Indexer (Ponder) feeds the Lexidex, animation states, and leaderboards. Notification service for hunger and jackpot events.

## 9. Fairness & compliance posture

- All randomness commit-reveal; all odds published; all splits on-chain and inspectable.
- Failed rolls never destroy or downgrade assets — the roll is an upgrade *fee with probabilistic timing*, not a wager that can lose the principal.
- Jackpot eligibility requires holding + staking an asset, not buying a ticket per se; answer selection is provably pre-committed and team-tamper-proof.
- *(Open item: a legal sanity pass on the jackpot mechanic for key jurisdictions before Phase 3 — see Open Questions.)*

## 10. Requirements summary

**P0 (cannot ship without):** 1155 Letters with demand-mirrored odds + supply cap; commit-reveal mint and rolls; pity system; unique permanent Word claims with escrow; the Lexidex with live claim status; fee routing per split table; staking with weights + hunger gating; snack burn; jackpot with rollover; LHAW answer hash chain; dual-surface app; hunger notifications.
**P1 (fast follow):** dissolution; Trophy variants; Remotion share clips; streak snacks; gifting between Farcaster mutuals; global completion stats and leaderboards.
**P2 (design for, don't build):** rarity-scaled roll fees; Rive state machines; seasons; uppercase-claim bonus events; marketplace deep-links inside the Lexidex.

Sample acceptance criteria (style for the rest): *Given a word is staked and unfed for 3 consecutive days, when the daily distribution runs, then that word accrues zero yield and its animation state is `hungry`; when its owner feeds it, then yield accrual resumes at the next distribution and the state transitions through `eating` to `idle`.*

## 11. Success metrics

**Leading (first 30 days):** unique minting wallets (target 1,000+); packs/day; % of mints that lead to a claim within 7 days (target 40%); claims (target 440 = 10% of dictionary); D7 retention of stakers (target 35%).
**Lagging (90 days):** % of LHAW actives holding Lexigotchi assets (target 25%); roll volume trend (the revenue engine); Rewards Pool inflow vs. outflow ratio (must stay > 1); jackpot cast engagement; secondary volume and floor by tier.
