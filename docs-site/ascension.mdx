**Ascension** is for a word you’ve finished. Once all five of its letters are UPPERCASE, you can climb it up the **word ladder**: four gilded stages, then **Holographic**, the top. Each stage raises the word’s share of [daily earnings](/earning) and the [weekly bounty](/bounty). The first four wrap more gold around its letters, and Holographic turns that gold into iridescent foil.

![An UPPERCASE W climbing from plain UPPERCASE through Gilded 1, 2, 3, and 4, then Holographic, with its share at each stage](/images/ascension.svg)

## Which words can ascend

You can try to ascend a word that is:

- yours,
- [staked](/care#staking), and
- all UPPERCASE. See [raising letters](/rolls).

## Each attempt

Each stage is harder and costs more than the last. A try also burns **\$0.02 in \$WORD**. That is never free: only the first word you wake each day is.

| Stage you’re trying for | First try | Each attempt costs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gilded 1 | 40% | \$0.50 in \$WORD |
| Gilded 2 | 35% | \$1.00 in \$WORD |
| Gilded 3 | 30% | \$1.50 in \$WORD |
| Gilded 4 | 25% | \$2.00 in \$WORD |
| Holographic | 20% | \$2.50 in \$WORD |

- A **miss** never harms the word. It raises that word’s odds by **10 points** for its next attempt, up to 85%.
- A **hit** moves the word up one stage. Its odds then start at the next stage’s first try.
- One attempt per word runs at a time. You confirm the attempt in your wallet once, and the game finishes it and shows you the result.

## How to try

Open the word and tap **Ascend**. The word stands up with its stage, the odds of this try, and what the try costs. Tap **Try for** the next stage.

While the attempt is out, the word’s letters can’t look. Then the result reaches them one by one:

- On a **hit**, each letter opens its eyes in its new gold, and the word cheers.
- On a **miss**, the word stays as it was, and its new odds show at once.

You can try again right there, as many times as you like. The button always shows the price of the next try.

On average, a word takes about 12 attempts and about \$20 in \$WORD, that \$0.02 a try included, to go from UPPERCASE to Holographic.

<Note>
  Keep the word staked until the result shows. If it’s unstaked or changes hands first, the attempt ends without a stage.
</Note>

## What each stage is worth

Each stage multiplies the word’s share by 1.1:

| Stage | Share of earnings and the bounty |
| --- | --- |
| UPPERCASE | 1× |
| Gilded 1 | 1.10× |
| Gilded 2 | 1.21× |
| Gilded 3 | 1.33× |
| Gilded 4 | 1.46× |
| Holographic | 1.61× |

This stacks with the word’s [rarity](/words#rarity). A Holographic Mythic word takes about 12.9 times a Common word’s share. The [daily jackpot](/jackpot) doesn’t change: every word of yours that is staked and awake is in the running, the same as any other.

## The stages stay with the word

A word’s stage and its odds belong to the word, not to you. They stay when you unstake it, and they go with it if it changes hands.

[Dissolving](/words#dissolving-a-word) a word clears its stages. Its five letters come back UPPERCASE, but its gold and foil are gone.

## Where the fee goes

An attempt’s fee splits like every paid action: 75% to the Rewards Pool, 10% to the jackpot, 10% burned, and 5% to the treasury. The extra \$0.02 is burned. See [where fees go](/fees).
