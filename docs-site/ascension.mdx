</Note>

**Ascension** is for a word you’ve finished. Once all five of its letters are UPPERCASE, you can gild it through **four stages**. Each stage raises the word’s share of [daily earnings](/earning) and the [weekly bounty](/bounty), and wraps more gold around its letters.

![An UPPERCASE W ascending from plain UPPERCASE through Gilded 1, 2, 3, and 4, with its share at each stage](/images/ascension.svg)

## Which words can ascend

You can try to ascend a word that is:

- yours,
- [staked](/care#staking), and
- all UPPERCASE. See [raising letters](/rolls).

## Each attempt

An attempt costs **\$0.25 in \$WORD**, the same as a roll, plus **one snack** (\$0.02 in \$WORD), which is burned. Your free daily snack doesn’t cover it.

| Attempt | Odds |
| --- | --- |
| First try | 45% |
| After each miss on that word | 10 points higher |
| Highest | 85% |

- A **hit** moves the word up one stage, and its odds go back to 45%.
- A **miss** never harms the word. It only raises the odds on that word’s next attempt.
- One attempt per word runs at a time. You confirm it in your wallet once, and the game shows you the result.

<Note>
  Keep the word staked until the result shows. If it’s unstaked or changes hands first, the attempt ends without a stage.
</Note>

## What each stage is worth

Each stage multiplies the word’s share by 1.1:

| Stage | Share of earnings and the bounty |
| --- | --- |
| UPPERCASE | 1× |
| Gilded 1 | 1.10× |
| Gilded 2 | 1.21× |
| Gilded 3 | 1.33× |
| Gilded 4 | 1.46× |

This stacks with the word’s [rarity](/words#rarity). A Gilded 4 Legendary word takes about 11.7 times a Common word’s share. The [daily jackpot](/jackpot) doesn’t change: every staked, fed word is still one ticket.

## The gold stays with the word

A word’s stage and its odds belong to the word, not to you. They stay when you unstake it, and they go with it if it changes hands.

[Dissolving](/words#dissolving-a-word) a word clears its gold. Its five letters come back UPPERCASE, but its stages are gone.

## Where the fee goes

The attempt fee splits the same way as a roll’s, and the snack is burned. See [where fees go](/fees).
