---
title: "Raising letters"
description: "Roll a letter to raise it from lowercase to UPPERCASE."
---

A **roll** tries to raise a lowercase letter to UPPERCASE. Each roll costs **\$0.25 in \$WORD**.

## Your odds

| Roll | Odds |
| --- | --- |
| First try | 45% |
| After each miss on that letter | 10 points higher |
| Highest | 85% |

Your odds reset to 45% after a hit. On average, a letter takes about **1.9 rolls** to raise.

<Note>
  A miss **never burns or downgrades** your letter. It only raises your odds on the next roll.
</Note>

## What you can roll

- **A loose letter** in your bag. Tap it in the **Bag** tab.
- **A letter inside a word you own.** Open the word and tap one of its lowercase letters. This is how a word becomes all-UPPERCASE.

One roll per letter runs at a time. You confirm the roll in your wallet once, and the game finishes it and shows you the result.

## Why UPPERCASE matters

An UPPERCASE letter is the blue game tile. Five UPPERCASE letters can spell an all-UPPERCASE word, and only all-UPPERCASE words [earn \$WORD](/earning).
