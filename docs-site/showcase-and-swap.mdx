## Showcase

A **showcase** arranges any letters you own into any string, with no dictionary check, and shares it on X or Farcaster. The link shows your letters as a picture card. Nothing is minted or spent.

![The Showcase tab in practice mode: the letters g, r, e, a, and t arranged into GREAT, ready to post on X or cast on Farcaster](/images/screen-showcase.jpg)

## Swap

A **swap** trades one of your letters for one of someone else’s. You list the offer and share it, and any player who holds the letter you want can take it. Each offer on the board shows the player who made it, with their name and picture.

- **Your offers** sit on the board too, marked **Yours**, including offers from your [linked wallets](/profile#linked-wallets). Cancel one with the wallet that listed it.
- **To take an offer**, the wallet you’re connected with must hold the letter they want. If another of your wallets holds it, switch to that wallet or [move the letter here](/profile#move-letters-between-wallets).
- **The board only shows offers that can go through.** An offer leaves the board while its maker no longer holds the letter, and comes back if they get it back.
