## Showcase

A **showcase** arranges any letters you own into any string, with no dictionary check, and shares it on X or Farcaster. The link shows your letters as a picture card. Nothing is minted or spent.

![The Showcase tab in practice mode: the letters g, r, e, a, and t arranged into GREAT, ready to post on X or cast on Farcaster](/images/screen-showcase.jpg)

## Swap

A **swap** trades one of your letters for one of someone else’s. You list the offer and share it, and any player who holds the letter you want can take it. You can cancel an offer that nobody has taken.
