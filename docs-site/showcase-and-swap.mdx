## Showcase

A **showcase** shows off one of your words, standing in its scene, and shares it on X or Farcaster. Pick a word you own, pick its scene, then post it. The link shows your word in that scene as a picture card. Only words you own can be shown, and nothing is minted or spent.

X adds the picture once it reads the link. The first time a word and scene are shared, X’s post box may not show the picture yet. Post anyway: the picture comes when X has read the link.

To keep the picture, tap **Save image**. On a phone, the share sheet opens: choose **Save Image** to put it in your camera roll. On a computer, it downloads. Inside the Farcaster or Base app, the picture may open in your browser instead: press and hold it to save it.

## Scenes

Every word has a home scene. It stands there on the **Today** tab and in your showcase.

- **Everyone has The Stage**: red curtains, footlights, and a spotlight.
- **Own 3 words to unlock the rest**: Trophy Case, Picture Day, Spring, Summer, Fall, and Winter. Once you’ve held 3 words, they stay unlocked, even if you dissolve or sell one later.

To change a word’s scene, tap the palette button under the stage on **Today**, or pick one in the Showcase. Each word keeps its own scene.

If you’re signed in to your [profile](/profile), your scenes are saved to it, so they follow you to every device and into Farcaster. If you’re not, they’re saved in this browser, and they move to your profile the next time you sign in. Choosing a scene never asks you to sign in.

A scene shows how its word is doing, too. When a word is peckish, its scene fades toward grey and a light fog drifts in. When it’s hungry, the color is almost gone and thick fog rolls over the scene. Feed it, and the scene clears.

## Sell a word

You can sell a word to another player for \$WORD. Open the word in your bag, tap **Sell**, and set a price. Listing is a free signature. The buyer pays your price: you get 90%, and 10% goes to the [Rewards Pool](/fees), which pays staked words.

- **Unstake it first.** Only a word in your wallet can be sold. While it’s for sale, it earns nothing and has no jackpot entry.
- **Everything in it goes with it**: its five letters, its case, its prestige, and its ascension pity.
- **To change the price**, set a new one. The game cancels your old listing in the same step, so nobody can buy at the old price. **Take off sale** cancels it.
- **A listing stops** when the word leaves your wallet (you stake it, move it, or dissolve it), and after 30 days.
- **To buy**, find it under **Words for sale** on the Swap screen and tap **Buy**. Your wallet approves exactly the price, and the word arrives in the wallet you’re connected with.

## Swap

A **swap** trades one of your letters for one of someone else’s. You list the offer and share it, and any player who holds the letter you want can take it. Each offer on the board shows the player who made it, with their name and picture.

- **Your offers** sit on the board too, marked **Yours**, including offers from your [linked wallets](/profile#linked-wallets). Cancel one with the wallet that listed it.
- **To take an offer**, the wallet you’re connected with must hold the letter they want. If another of your wallets holds it, switch to that wallet or [move the letter here](/profile#move-letters-between-wallets).
- **The board only shows offers that can go through.** An offer leaves the board while its maker no longer holds the letter, and comes back if they get it back.
