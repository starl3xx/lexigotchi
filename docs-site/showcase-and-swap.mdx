## Share a word

Show off one of your words, standing in its scene, on X or Farcaster. Open the word (tap it on **Today**, or its card in your **Bag**), then tap **Share** under its card. The link shows your word in its scene as a picture card, its tiles in the finish of its [rarity](/words#rarity). Only words you own can be shared, and nothing is minted or spent.

X adds the picture once it reads the link. The first time a word and scene are shared, X’s post box may not show the picture yet. Post anyway: the picture comes when X has read the link.

To keep the picture, tap **Save image**. A saved picture travels without its link, so it carries the game’s name in a corner. On a phone, the share sheet opens: choose **Save Image** to put it in your camera roll. On a computer, it downloads. Inside the Farcaster or Base app, the picture may open in your browser instead: press and hold it to save it.

To share the card turning over, tap **Make a video of the card**. In a few seconds, your browser makes a six-second video: the word in its scene, the card turning over, its back catching the light, and home again, with the game’s name in a corner. On a phone, **Share video** sends it to X or anywhere else, or saves it. On a computer, **Save video** downloads it. On X, add the video to your post: a link shows only a still picture. Inside the Farcaster or Base app, the video is offered only where the app can share a file.

## Scenes

Every word has a home scene. It stands there on the **Today** tab, on its card in your **Bag**, and in the pictures you share.

- **Everyone has The Stage**: red curtains, footlights, and a spotlight.
- **Own 3 words to unlock the rest**: Trophy Case, Picture Day, Spring, Summer, Fall, and Winter. Once you’ve held 3 words, they stay unlocked, even if you dissolve or sell one later.

To change a word’s scene, tap the palette button under the stage on **Today**, or **Scene** under its card when you open it. Each word keeps its own scene.

If you’re signed in to your [profile](/profile), your scenes are saved to it, so they follow you to every device and into Farcaster. If you’re not, they’re saved in this browser, and they move to your profile the next time you sign in. Choosing a scene never asks you to sign in.

A scene shows how its word is doing, too. When a word is peckish, its scene fades toward grey and a light fog drifts in. When it’s hungry, the color is almost gone and thick fog rolls over the scene. Feed it, and the scene clears.

## Market

The **Market** is where players buy and sell words and letters for \$WORD. A sale happens the moment someone buys: the \$WORD goes to the seller, and the word or the letters go to the buyer, in one step.

It has four parts: **Words**, **Letters**, **Sell**, and **Sold**. Above them, **Just sold** shows the latest sales.

![The Market in practice mode: the latest sales across the top, three picks for the player (two of them a letter that finishes a word, drawn with its empty tile), and the letters shelf, each letter that’s for sale with a price tag under it](/images/screen-market.jpg)

### The best deals for you

The top of the Market shows up to three listings picked for you:

- **A letter that finishes a word.** If your letters are one short of a word nobody owns, and someone is selling that letter, the Market says so: “A z finishes BLAZE”, or “One more z finishes JAZZY” when you hold one z and the word has two. It names the rarest such word, draws it with an empty dashed tile where the letter goes, and shows the lowest price for the letter. **and 45 more words** opens the [Dictionary](/words) on every word that letter finishes.
- **Something listed for less than it costs to make.** A letter or a word priced at least 10% under what it would cost you to make today. A word is only picked if you have enough \$WORD for it.

Your own listings are never picked. The Market counts your letters as the Dictionary does: only the letters in the wallet you’re connected with, and never a letter you already hold in a [linked wallet](/profile#linked-wallets), because you can [move that one](/profile#move-letters-between-wallets) instead of buying another.

### Buy a word

Open **Words**. Every word for sale is in the list, shown as it is right now: its case, its [ascension](/ascension) stage, and how long it has gone unfed.

- **Find one.** Type letters to find a word. Show one rarity, only **All UPPERCASE** words (they [earn \$WORD](/earning) once staked), or only words **Within my \$WORD**.
- **Sort.** Best deal first (the lowest price against what the word costs to make), lowest or highest price, newest, or rarest first.
- **Compare.** Each word shows its price against what it costs to make (“18% under what it costs to make”), what it last sold for, about what it would earn a day staked, and whether the [jackpot](/jackpot) has already drawn it. The locked order of secret words never repeats one, so a word that has been drawn won’t be drawn again.
- **Open it.** Tap a word to open it, as in the Dictionary: its owner, its card number, its rarity and rank among all the words, its points, and its price against what it costs to make. Its card number also shows in the list.
- **Buy.** Tap **Buy**, in the list or in the opened word. Your wallet approves exactly the price, and the word arrives in the wallet you’re connected with, with its five letters, its case, its ascension, and its ascension pity.

When the word arrives, it stands up in its scene, with what you paid under it. From there you can **stake it** or **see its card**.

### Buy letters

Open **Letters**. The whole alphabet stands on a shelf. A letter that’s for sale has a price tag under it: the lowest price, and how many are for sale. However many players are selling, you read 26 tags.

Tap a letter to see who’s selling it, lowercase and UPPERCASE apart, lowest price first. Each seller’s row shows the price for one, how many they have, and that price against what the letter costs to make. Pick how many you want and tap **Buy**. You can take one of a seller’s letters or all of them.

If that letter finishes a word for you, its sellers say so, with the word and a link to every word it finishes. **Find a q in the Market**, in a word you opened in the [Dictionary](/words), brings you straight here, to that letter’s sellers.

When the letters arrive, the game shows them with what you paid. If they finish a word nobody owns, it names the rarest one, and you can claim it right there.

What a letter costs to make is its share of a pack: a pack’s price split across its five letters, so a rare letter like q costs more. An UPPERCASE letter adds the average number of [rolls](/rolls) it takes to raise one.

### Sell a word

You can sell a word to another player for \$WORD. Open **Sell** in the Market and choose **Your words**, or open the word in your bag and tap **Sell**. Set a price. Listing is a free signature. The buyer pays your price: you get 90%, and 10% goes to the [Rewards Pool](/fees), which pays staked words.

- **Unstake it first.** Only a word in your wallet can be sold. While it’s for sale, it earns nothing and isn’t in the running for the jackpot.
- **Prices to start from.** The sell form offers prices, each with its reason, and one tap fills it in: about what the word costs to make, what it last sold for, what words of its rarity have sold for lately, and the lowest word of its rarity for sale now.
- **What it costs to make.** About what the word would cost to build today, at today’s prices and with the average number of tries: its letters, the rolls that raised its UPPERCASE letters, the claim, and each [ascension](/ascension) stage it reached. It isn’t what you paid. The form tells you if your price is lower.
- **What it earns.** For an all-UPPERCASE word, the form shows about how much \$WORD it would earn a day, staked and fed, from today’s [Rewards Pool](/earning).
- **Everything in it goes with it**: its five letters, its case, its ascension, and its ascension pity.
- **To change the price**, tap **Change the price** and set a new one. The game cancels your old listing in the same step, so nobody can buy at the old price. **Take off sale** cancels it.
- **A listing stops** when the word leaves your wallet (you stake it, move it, or dissolve it), and after 30 days.
- **In your bag**, a word for sale shows **for sale** and its price, and the **For sale** filter lists them. Today stops asking you to stake or raise it.

### Sell letters

Open **Sell** in the Market, choose **Your letters**, and tap a letter. The **Letters** side of your Bag has a shortcut: **Sell letters on the Market**. Say how many to sell and the price for each. You can list one letter or several of the same letter at once, such as five w’s, and a buyer can take any number of them. Listing is a free signature. For each letter sold you get 90%, and 10% goes to the [Rewards Pool](/fees).

- **Prices to start from.** The form offers a price that makes yours the lowest for sale, what the last one sold for, and about what the letter costs to make. It also shows your price against the lowest for sale now.
- **Listed letters stay in your bag** until they sell. You can still spell with them, roll them, or move them: the listing gets smaller.
- **A listing pauses** when the wallet holds none of that letter. It goes back on sale by itself, at the same price, if the wallet holds one again. **Take off sale** stops it for good.
- **One listing for each letter.** To change the price or how many, tap **Change**: the game cancels your old listing in the same step.
- **A listing ends** after 30 days.
- **Only the wallet that holds a letter can list it.** If your letters are in another of your wallets, switch to it, or [move them here](/profile#move-letters-between-wallets).
- **To close a sell form** without listing, tap the × in its corner, or **Cancel**.

### Your listings

**Sell** shows everything you have for sale, from every wallet in your [profile](/profile#linked-wallets), paused listings included. Take one off sale with the wallet that listed it.

### What sold

**Sold** lists recent sales, newest first: the word or the letters, what the buyer paid, who sold to whom, and when. The game reads sales from the chain, so every sale shows there.

<Note>
  Letter swaps, one letter for another, have closed. Letters sell for \$WORD now. If you made a swap offer before, it’s under **Sell**, and it can still be taken until you cancel it or it runs out, 30 days after you made it.
</Note>
