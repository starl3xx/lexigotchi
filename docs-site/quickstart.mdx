---
title: "Start playing"
description: "Connect a wallet, get your first letters, and claim your first word."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the game">
    Go to [lexigotchi.fun/play](https://lexigotchi.fun/play). Lexigotchi also runs inside Farcaster and the Base App.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a wallet">
    Your wallet is your Lexigotchi account. There’s no sign-up. An email is optional, only for [reminders](/care#reminders). Connect any browser wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, Phantom, Coinbase Wallet, and more), or tap **Sign in with Coinbase Wallet**. Inside Farcaster, the game uses your Farcaster wallet (during the testnet beta, if it supports Base Sepolia). During the beta your wallet needs Base Sepolia: the game asks it to switch, and tells you if it’s on another network. Some wallets, like Rainbow, don’t offer Base Sepolia: play the test with MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your first letters">
    Take your [free letters](/letters#free-letters): link Farcaster, X, or a Coinbase-verified wallet in [your profile](/profile) for a free pack of 5 plus 10 free daily letters. Or buy a [pack of five](/letters#packs) for \$1 in \$WORD.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get some $WORD">
    Packs, rolls, claims, and snacks are paid in \$WORD. Tap your balance in the game’s header, then **Buy \$WORD**. See [\$WORD and prices](/word).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spell a word">
    Open **Spell**, pick five letters that make a dictionary word (any mix of lowercase and UPPERCASE), and claim it for \$0.50 in \$WORD. The word is yours alone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stake it and keep it fed">
    Stake the word and keep it fed: a staked and fed word is in the running for the [daily jackpot](/jackpot). Feed it when it gets peckish. See [staking and feeding](/care).
  </Step>
</Steps>

![The Today tab in practice mode: the card of your hungriest word, its face grey and foggy, with its three status marks, a Feed button, and arrows to your other words under it; your letters, with today’s free letter, Spell, and Mint; and your next steps](/images/screen-today.jpg)

<Tip>
  **Cheat sheet** in the side menu (on a phone, the **?** in the header) opens a one-screen cheat sheet of the whole loop. The **Today** tab is your daily dashboard: your words first, starting with the one that needs you most, then your letters, today’s jackpot, and your next steps.
</Tip>
