Each week has a **theme**. When the week ends, every word that matches the theme and is **staked and not hungry** at that moment shares the bounty pool. Rarer words take a bigger slice, by their [tier weight](/words#rarity).

## The themes

The themes take turns, one per week:

- Contains a rare letter (Q, Z, X, or J)
- Has a repeated letter
- Ends in -ING
- Starts with a vowel
- Ends in Y

The **Bounty** tab shows this week’s theme, your matching words, and when the week ends.

## Getting paid

The bounty pays on the first day of the next week. Collect it in the game, alongside your other [rewards](/earning#collecting-your-rewards). If no word matches in a week, the pool carries over to the next one.

## Where the pool comes from

**15%** of the Rewards Pool’s share of every fee goes to the bounty. See [where fees go](/fees).
