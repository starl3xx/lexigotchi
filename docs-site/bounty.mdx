Each week has a **theme**. When the week ends, every word that matches the theme and is **staked and not hungry** at that moment shares the bounty pool. Rarer words take a bigger slice, by their [tier weight](/words#rarity), and so do words that have [ascended](/ascension).

![The Jackpot tab in practice mode: today’s jackpot on the left; on the right, this week’s bounty theme and pool, your matching words, and the bounty leaderboard](/images/screen-bounty.jpg)

## The themes

The themes take turns, one per week:

- Contains a rare letter (Q, Z, X, or J)
- Has a repeated letter
- Ends in -ING
- Starts with a vowel
- Ends in Y

The **Jackpot** tab shows the bounty next to today’s jackpot: this week’s theme, your matching words, and when the week ends. The **Today** tab names the theme too. Tap it to go straight to the bounty.

## Getting paid

The bounty pays on the first day of the next week, straight to the wallet that holds each winning word, like your [daily earnings](/earning#getting-paid). There’s nothing to claim. If no word matches in a week, the pool carries over to the next one.

## Where the pool comes from

**15%** of the Rewards Pool’s share of every fee goes to the bounty. See [where fees go](/fees).
