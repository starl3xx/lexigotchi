---
title: "FAQ"
description: "Quick answers to common questions."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a Farcaster account?">
    No. Your wallet is your account: connect any wallet and play. Linking Farcaster is optional. The [free daily letter](/letters#the-daily-letter) needs proof you’re one person: a linked Farcaster account, a linked X account older than 90 days, or a Coinbase-verified wallet. See [your profile](/profile).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I linked Farcaster. Do I still need a wallet?">
    Yes. Your wallet is your Lexigotchi account: your letters and words live in it. Linking Farcaster on the web unlocks the free daily letter and your name and picture, but it doesn’t connect a wallet. Tap **Connect wallet**, or **Sign in with Coinbase Wallet**. Inside the Farcaster app, the game uses your Farcaster wallet instead. During the testnet beta that only works if your Farcaster wallet supports Base Sepolia; if it doesn’t, the game says so, and you connect another wallet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need an email?">
    No. An email is optional, only for [reminders](/care#reminders). It’s never shown to anyone, and every reminder email has a one-click unsubscribe link. See [email reminders](/profile#email-reminders).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get a name and picture?">
    Link Farcaster or X in [your profile](/profile). Your name and picture come only from a linked account. If you link both, you choose which one names you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is my linked X account public?">
    Yes. Anyone can see which X account is linked to your wallet, and the profile says so next to the **Link** button. Lexigotchi reads only your own X profile, once. It never posts for you and keeps no access to your X account, even though X’s approval screen asks to see posts (X requires that to read any profile). You can unlink anytime, except on a day the X account took the free letter. See [your profile](/profile#x).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I play on the web and in Farcaster. Is my bag shared?">
    Yes. Your bag shows your connected wallet plus every wallet your Farcaster account has verified. If you play on the web with a wallet Farcaster hasn’t verified, verify it in Farcaster, or link it in your profile, and your whole bag shows in both places. See [your bag](/letters#your-bag).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which wallet should I play with?">
    One wallet, your [game wallet](/profile#your-game-wallet). Your letters, words, and \$WORD are onchain, and the game can only use what the connected wallet holds. If you use more than one wallet, choose a game wallet in your profile and move everything there. Inside Farcaster you can only use your Farcaster wallet, so if you play mostly there, choose it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I feed my staked words from another wallet?">
    Yes, once the staking wallet names that wallet a [caretaker](/profile#caretakers-feed-from-any-of-your-wallets) in your profile. A caretaker can feed, roll, and ascend those words and pays from its own wallet. Only the staking wallet can unstake them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I play with more than one wallet?">
    Yes. Link your other wallets in [your profile](/profile#linked-wallets), up to five. Wallets your Farcaster account verified join on their own. They become one account: one bag, one profile, and one free daily letter. Letters and words stay in the wallet that holds them, so you play with the wallet you’re connected with, or [move them](/profile#move-letters-between-wallets) into one wallet. The links are public, and the leaderboard counts your wallets as one player.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a roll cost me my letter?">
    No. A missed roll never burns or downgrades a letter. It only raises your odds on the next roll. See [raising letters](/rolls).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can two people own the same word?">
    No. Every word has exactly one NFT, so it has one owner at a time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I stop feeding a word?">
    After a day it gets peckish and earns half. After three days it’s hungry: out of the jackpot and earning nothing until you feed it. See [staking and feeding](/care).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I get my letters back from a word?">
    Yes. Dissolve the word to get its five letters back in their current case. The word becomes free for anyone to claim. See [words](/words#dissolving-a-word).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do lowercase words earn anything?">
    A staked, fed word of any case is in the daily jackpot and can share the weekly bounty. Only all-UPPERCASE words earn the daily \$WORD share. See [earning \$WORD](/earning).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I see what my words have earned? Do I have to claim it?">
    There’s nothing to claim: every day at 00:05 UTC, each earning word’s share is paid straight to the wallet that holds it. The line under your words on the **Today** tab says what the latest payout brought. Tap it, or the earnings row on any word, to see each word’s total. See [earning \$WORD](/earning#seeing-what-your-words-earned).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the jackpot fair?">
    Yes. The order of secret words was locked onchain before launch, so nobody can steer it. See [fairness](/fairness).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How much does it cost to play?">
    The daily letter is free. A pack is \$1, a roll \$0.25, a claim \$0.50, and a snack \$0.02, all paid in \$WORD. See [\$WORD and prices](/word).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many words and letters are there?">
    4,584 words, 46 of them Legendary, and 57,294 letters in all. The dictionary is Let’s Have A Word!’s, and it grows when theirs does.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is ascension?">
    Ascension gilds a staked, all-UPPERCASE word through four stages, and each stage raises its share of earnings and the bounty. A miss never harms the word. It isn’t switched on yet. See [ascension](/ascension).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I know when a word is getting hungry?">
    Turn on reminders. The game tells you before a staked word goes hungry, when your free daily letter is ready, and when you win the jackpot. Email reminders are on. Reminders inside Farcaster and in your browser aren’t switched on yet. See [reminders](/care#reminders).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the game live?">
    Lexigotchi runs on Base Sepolia, a test network, until its contracts launch on Base. Until then, the \$WORD in the game is test \$WORD with no value. Tap your balance and **Get test \$WORD** from the faucet. If your wallet pays its own gas, it needs a little Base Sepolia ETH, which any Base Sepolia faucet gives out free.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
