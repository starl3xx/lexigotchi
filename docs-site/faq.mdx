---
title: "FAQ"
description: "Quick answers to common questions."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a Farcaster account?">
    No. Your wallet is your account: connect any wallet and play. Linking Farcaster is optional. The [free daily letter](/letters#the-daily-letter) needs proof you’re one person, which a linked Farcaster account or a Coinbase-verified wallet gives you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I play on the web and in Farcaster. Is my bag shared?">
    Yes. Your bag shows your connected wallet plus every wallet your Farcaster account has verified. If you play on the web with a wallet Farcaster hasn’t verified, verify it in Farcaster and your whole bag shows in both places. See [your bag](/letters#your-bag).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a roll cost me my letter?">
    No. A missed roll never burns or downgrades a letter. It only raises your odds on the next roll. See [raising letters](/rolls).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can two people own the same word?">
    No. Every word has exactly one NFT, so it has one owner at a time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I stop feeding a word?">
    After a day it gets peckish and earns half. After three days it’s hungry: out of the jackpot and earning nothing until you feed it. See [staking and feeding](/care).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I get my letters back from a word?">
    Yes. Dissolve the word to get its five letters back in their current case. The word becomes free for anyone to claim. See [words](/words#dissolving-a-word).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do lowercase words earn anything?">
    A staked, fed word of any case is in the daily jackpot and can share the weekly bounty. Only all-UPPERCASE words earn the daily \$WORD share. See [earning \$WORD](/earning).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the jackpot fair?">
    Yes. The order of secret words was locked onchain before launch, so nobody can steer it. See [fairness](/fairness).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How much does it cost to play?">
    The daily letter is free. A pack is \$1, a roll \$0.25, a claim \$0.50, and a snack \$0.02, all paid in \$WORD. See [\$WORD and prices](/word).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many words and letters are there?">
    4,584 words, 46 of them Legendary, and 57,294 letters in all. The dictionary is Let’s Have A Word!’s, and it grows when theirs does.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is ascension?">
    Ascension gilds a staked, all-UPPERCASE word through four stages, and each stage raises its share of earnings and the bounty. A miss never harms the word. It isn’t switched on yet. See [ascension](/ascension).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I know when a word is getting hungry?">
    Turn on reminders. The game tells you before a staked word goes hungry, when your free daily letter is ready, and when you win the jackpot. Reminders aren’t switched on yet. See [reminders](/care#reminders).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the game live?">
    Lexigotchi is in practice mode until its contracts launch on Base. Until then, balances and prices in the game are for practice.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
