---
title: "Sets"
description: "Some words are held back in themed sets that unlock later."
---

Not every word in the dictionary can be claimed from day one. Some are held back in **sets**: groups of words with a theme, which unlock over time. Until a set unlocks, nobody can claim its words.

Open the **Dictionary** to see the sets. While a set is news (revealed, just unlocked, or in its first day), **Today** shows it too, and a tap there opens the set in the Dictionary.

## A locked set

A locked slot says **A new set is coming**, and nothing more. Its name and its words stay secret until the set is revealed.

## A revealed set

A revealed set shows its **name**, its **words**, and a countdown to when it unlocks. You can’t claim its words yet, so this is the time to get ready. Tap a word to see which of its letters you have and which you still need, with the ways to get them: a pack, or the [Market](/showcase-and-swap#market).

Once a set is revealed, its words don’t change.

## An unlocked set

When a set unlocks, its words are words like any others. Spell one with five of your letters and [claim it](/words): it’s yours alone. A word from a set carries the set’s name on the back of its [card](/words#word-cards), for good.

For a day after it unlocks, a set stays near the top of the Dictionary with its words showing, and on Today. After that it folds to its name.

A set that has unlocked never locks again.

## The first day

For the first day after a set unlocks, each player can claim **one word** from it. After that, every word that’s left is open to everyone.

- **One word per player**, not per wallet: your linked wallets count as one player.
- **Verified players only** on the first day: link Farcaster, an X account older than 90 days, or a Coinbase-verified wallet in your [profile](/profile). It’s the same check as for your free letters.
- The set shows **First day: one word per player** and counts down to when it opens to everyone.

The first day keeps one player from taking a whole set in its first minute.

<Note>
  On the test build, the first-day limit starts with the next test reset. Until then, a set that unlocks is open to everyone at once.
</Note>

## Before a word unlocks

- **Spell** tells you a held-back word isn’t available yet.
- **Its letters already exist.** Letters are made for the whole dictionary from the start, so a set’s words need no new letters, and a word’s [rarity](/words#rarity) is the same before and after it unlocks.
- **Counts follow the words you can claim.** Where the game says how much of the dictionary is claimed, it counts the words that have unlocked.

## Sets and the jackpot

The [daily word](/jackpot) list was fixed before the game began, and it takes no account of sets. So the day’s word can be one that hasn’t unlocked yet. Nobody can hold that word, so the pot **rolls over** that day, as it does for any word nobody holds.

Each word is the daily word once, ever. A word that comes up before its set unlocks has had its day.

## Reminders

Your [reminders](/profile#email-reminders) tell you when a set is revealed, when it unlocks, and when its first day ends. A tap on one opens the sets in the Dictionary. To stop them, turn off **A new set of words** under **What to tell me** in your profile.
