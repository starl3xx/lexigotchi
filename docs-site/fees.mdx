---
title: "Where fees go"
description: "Every fee is split between burning, rewards, the jackpot, and the treasury."
---

Every fee you pay is split four ways:

- **Burned**: gone for good.
- **Rewards Pool**: pays [daily earnings](/earning), and 15% of its share funds the [weekly bounty](/bounty).
- **Jackpot**: the [daily jackpot](/jackpot) pot.
- **Treasury**: a small reserve for the game.

![Where each fee goes, as one bar per fee: pack, roll, claim, ascension, and waking a word](/images/fees.svg)

| Fee | Burned | Rewards Pool | Jackpot | Treasury |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pack, roll, claim, or ascension | 10% | 75% | 10% | 5% |
| Waking a word | 100% | | | |
| Market sale fee | | 100% | | |

Every paid action splits the same way, so most of what players spend goes back to staked, awake words, as [daily earnings](/earning), the [weekly bounty](/bounty), and the [jackpot](/jackpot). What you pay to wake a word is burned, and so is the extra \$0.02 on each [ascension](/ascension) attempt, so waking words takes \$WORD out for good. The daily letter is free, so it adds nothing to any of them. When a word or a letter [sells on the Market](/showcase-and-swap#market), 10% of its price is the fee, and all of it goes to the Rewards Pool.

<Note>
  The pools can never pay out more than they hold. Earnings are a share of what’s in the Rewards Pool, and the jackpot pays what’s in the pot.
</Note>
