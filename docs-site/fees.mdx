---
title: "Where fees go"
description: "Every fee is split between burning, rewards, the jackpot, and the treasury."
---

Every fee you pay is split four ways:

- **Burned**: gone for good.
- **Rewards Pool**: pays [daily earnings](/earning), and 15% of its share funds the [weekly bounty](/bounty).
- **Jackpot**: the [daily jackpot](/jackpot) pot.
- **Treasury**: runs the game.

![Where each fee goes, as one bar per fee: pack, roll, claim, and snack](/images/fees.svg)

| Fee | Burned | Rewards Pool | Jackpot | Treasury |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pack | 20% | 40% | 10% | 30% |
| Roll | 27.5% | 47.5% | 10% | 15% |
| Claim | 32.5% | 32.5% | 10% | 25% |
| Snack | 100% | | | |

The daily letter is free, so it adds nothing to any of them. An [ascension](/ascension) attempt splits like a roll, and its snack is burned.

<Note>
  The pools can never pay out more than they hold. Earnings are a share of what’s in the Rewards Pool, and the jackpot pays what’s in the pot.
</Note>
