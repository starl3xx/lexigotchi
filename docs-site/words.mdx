## Claiming a word

Spell a word from the dictionary with **any five of your letters, in any mix of cases**: all lowercase, all UPPERCASE, or some of each. Claiming it costs **\$0.50 in \$WORD**.

When you claim, your five letters lock inside the word, and the word is yours alone. Each word has exactly one owner, so nobody else can own it while you do.

The dictionary is the same 4,584 five-letter words as [Let’s Have A Word!](/lets-have-a-word). The **Dictionary** tab shows every word and who owns it. Search it, show only unclaimed or claimed words, or show one rarity at a time.

The **Spell** tab lists every unclaimed word your letters can spell right now, rarest first. Switch to A–Z, or show one rarity at a time. A word another player already owns isn’t listed, because only one player can own it.

A word claimed from the list uses your UPPERCASE letters wherever you have them, so it starts as close to all-UPPERCASE as your bag allows. To choose exactly which letters go in, tap them into the rack yourself.

Spell shows all your letters, lowercase and UPPERCASE together. Filter them to **lowercase** or **UPPERCASE** to see one case alone, and the words that case spells by itself. **UPPERCASE** lists the words that would be all-UPPERCASE from the start, which earn \$WORD once staked.

## A word’s case

A word’s case comes from its letters. It starts in the case of the letters you spell it with:

- **lowercase**: none of its letters raised.
- **3/5 raised** (or 1/5, 2/5, 4/5): some raised.
- **UPPERCASE**: all five raised.

![The word crane three ways: lowercase, with three of its five letters raised, and all UPPERCASE](/images/word-case.svg)

You can [roll](/rolls) the letters inside a word you own. Only an all-UPPERCASE word [earns \$WORD](/earning) or can [ascend](/ascension).

## Rarity

Every word is scored by how rare its letters are and given one of five tiers. A rarer word takes a bigger share of [earnings](/earning) and the [weekly bounty](/bounty).

| Tier | Words | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common | 2,292 | 1 |
| Uncommon | 1,375 | 2 |
| Rare | 687 | 3 |
| Legendary | 184 | 5 |
| Mythic | 46 | 8 |

Every tier above Common has its own **finish** on its letter tiles: Uncommon is satin jade, Rare is sapphire foil, Legendary is polished gold, and Mythic is cut amethyst. Every few seconds, a light passes over the word you’re looking at. A word shows its finish in any case, in the game and on its share card. A peckish word’s shine dims, and a hungry word loses it until you feed it.

![The letter a in each tier’s finish: Common plain, Uncommon satin jade, Rare sapphire foil, Legendary polished gold, Mythic cut amethyst](/images/finishes.svg)

The rarest words are **Mythic**. JAZZY is the rarest of all.

## Word cards

Every word you own is a card. Its front is the word standing in its [scene](/showcase-and-swap#scenes). Tap the button in the card’s top right corner to turn it over. The back is in the material of the word’s rarity, and it tells the word’s story:

- **Its rarity**, and its number in the dictionary, A to Z.
- **Its case**, and its [ascension](/ascension) stage.
- **Yours since**: the day the word came to you. Moving it between your own wallets keeps the date.
- **Staked**, and how it’s doing: full, peckish, or hungry.
- **What it has earned you**, about what it [earns](/earning) a day staked, and about what it costs to make.
- **Its points**: its letters’ values added up.
- **Found by**: the first player ever to spell it. A word that was dissolved and spelled again keeps its first finder.
- **The jackpot**: whether it has been drawn yet, and its token on the block explorer.

On a phone, the back shows the first four. A bigger card shows them all.

Your **Bag** opens on your words, as cards. Turn one over right there, or tap it to open it. The opened card holds everything you can do with the word: feed it, stake it, raise its letters, ascend it, sell it, pick its scene, and [share it](/showcase-and-swap#share-a-word).

![The Bag in practice mode: your words as cards, each standing in its scene with its rarity in the corner, and its name and state underneath](/images/screen-cards.jpg)

## Dissolving a word

Changed your mind? **Dissolve** a word to unmake it and get its five letters back, in their current case. The word is then free for anyone to claim. Unstake it first: a staked word can’t be dissolved.

## Your word, onchain

Each claimed word lives onchain on Base, under the [ERC-721](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-721) standard for one-of-a-kind items. The collection is called **Lexigotchi Word** (symbol `WORD-NFT`).

- **One word, one ID, for good.** A word’s ID is the keccak256 hash of the word in capitals, so CRANE always has the same ID and can only ever exist once.
- **It holds its letters.** Your five letters move into the Words contract when you claim, and they stay there while the word exists. Raising a letter in a word swaps it for its UPPERCASE version inside the contract. The word’s case is read from those five letters, never stored on its own.
- **You can send or sell it.** [Sell it on the Market](/showcase-and-swap#sell-a-word) for \$WORD, send it to another wallet, or trade it anywhere on Base that supports ERC-721. Its letters, its case, and its [ascension](/ascension) go with it.
- **Except while it’s staked.** [Staking](/care) moves the word into the game’s Staking contract until you unstake. A staked word can’t be sent, sold, or dissolved.
- **Royalties.** Each word carries a 2.5% royalty signal for the game’s treasury, using [ERC-2981](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-2981). Other apps that trade words may honor it, but the contract doesn’t enforce it.
- **It changes over time.** Rolls and ascension change how a word looks. Each change emits an [ERC-4906](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-4906) event, which tells other apps to refresh their picture of it.

The contract addresses are on [contracts and standards](/contracts).
