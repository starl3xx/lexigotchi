## Claiming a word

Spell a word from the dictionary with **five letters of the same case**: all lowercase or all UPPERCASE. Claiming it costs **\$0.50 in \$WORD**.

When you claim, your five letters lock inside a **Word NFT**. There is exactly one NFT per word, so nobody else can own your word while you do.

The dictionary is the same 4,584 five-letter words as [Let’s Have A Word!](/lets-have-a-word). The **Dictionary** tab shows every word and who owns it.

The **Spell** tab lists every unclaimed word your letters can spell right now, rarest first. Switch to A–Z, or show one rarity at a time. A word another player already owns isn’t listed, because only one player can own it.

## A word’s case

A word’s case comes from its letters:

- **lowercase**: none of its letters raised.
- **3/5 raised** (or 1/5, 2/5, 4/5): some raised.
- **UPPERCASE**: all five raised.

![The word crane three ways: lowercase, with three of its five letters raised, and all UPPERCASE](/images/word-case.svg)

You can [roll](/rolls) the letters inside a word you own. Only an all-UPPERCASE word [earns \$WORD](/earning) or can [ascend](/ascension).

## Rarity

Every word is scored by how rare its letters are and given one of five tiers. A rarer word takes a bigger share of [earnings](/earning) and the [weekly bounty](/bounty).

| Tier | Words | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common | 2,292 | 1 |
| Uncommon | 1,375 | 2 |
| Rare | 687 | 3 |
| Epic | 184 | 5 |
| Legendary | 46 | 8 |

The rarest words are **crowns**. JAZZY is the rarest of all.

![JAZZY, the rarest crown, celebrating](/images/crown.svg)

## Dissolving a word

Changed your mind? **Dissolve** a word to burn its NFT and get its five letters back, in their current case. The word is then free for anyone to claim.
