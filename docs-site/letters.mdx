Letters are the characters you collect. Every letter arrives **lowercase**. The only way to get an UPPERCASE letter is to [roll](/rolls) one you own.

![A lowercase a, the way every letter arrives, and the same letter raised to UPPERCASE after a roll hits](/images/letters-cases.svg)

## The daily letter

You get **one free letter every day**. It resets at midnight UTC. Claim it each day: a day you skip doesn’t carry over, so letters never pile up for later. Once you claim it, **Today** and **Mint** both show the letter you got.

To unlock it, prove you’re one person in one of three ways. Tap your picture in the game’s header to open [your profile](/profile) and link one:

- Link a **Farcaster** account.
- Link an **X** account that’s older than 90 days.
- Use a [Coinbase-verified](https://www.coinbase.com/onchain-verify) wallet.

Link as many as you like: it’s still one letter a day. Everything else in the game works without any of them.

## Packs

A **pack of five** lowercase letters costs **\$1 in \$WORD**. You can buy packs anytime, as many as you like. The letters pop out of the pack one at a time.

## Which letters you get

A letter’s odds mirror the dictionary. The 4,584 words have 22,920 letters between them, and each letter’s chance in a draw is its share of those slots. So E, S, A, R, and O come up often. The rarest letters are the **grails**: V, Z, X, J, and Q.

![The five grails, the rarest letters: V, Z, X, J, and Q](/images/grails.svg)

Every letter also has a fixed supply: 2.5 times its share of the dictionary, or **57,294 letters** in all.

## Letter tokens

Letters are [ERC-1155](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-1155) tokens on Base, the standard for items you can hold several of. There are 52 token IDs: 0 to 25 are lowercase a to z, and 26 to 51 are UPPERCASE A to Z.

- **They’re yours to move.** Send letters to another wallet, [swap](/showcase-and-swap#swap) them with other players, or trade them anywhere that supports ERC-1155 tokens on Base.
- **The supply is fixed onchain.** The Letters contract enforces each letter’s cap. Once a letter’s supply has been minted, no more of it can be.
- **UPPERCASE comes only from rolls.** A successful [roll](/rolls) burns your lowercase letter and mints its UPPERCASE version. Only the game’s own contracts may do that, so an UPPERCASE letter can’t be minted any other way.

The contract addresses are on [contracts and standards](/contracts).

## Your bag

Your bag holds everything in your connected wallet, plus everything in the wallets your Farcaster account has verified, and in every wallet you’ve [linked in your profile](/profile#linked-wallets), shown together wherever you play.

![The Bag tab in practice mode: lowercase and UPPERCASE letters, with a count on each letter you hold more than one of](/images/screen-bag.jpg)

Two details:

- New letters always arrive in the **wallet that’s connected**.
- Spelling, rolling, staking, and feeding need the **wallet that holds** the letter or word. To gather everything in one wallet, choose a [game wallet](/profile#your-game-wallet) and [move it](/profile#move-letters-between-wallets) there.

<Tip>
  If you play on the web with a wallet your Farcaster account hasn’t verified, verify it in Farcaster (Settings, then Verified addresses). Your whole bag then shows in both places.
</Tip>
