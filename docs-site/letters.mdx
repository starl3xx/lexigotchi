Letters are the characters you collect. Every letter arrives **lowercase**. The only way to get an UPPERCASE letter is to [roll](/rolls) one you own.

![A lowercase a, the way every letter arrives, and the same letter raised to UPPERCASE after a roll hits](/images/letters-cases.svg)

## Free letters

Prove you’re one person and you get **a free pack of 5 plus 10 free daily letters**, once for your whole profile:

- **The free pack:** five lowercase letters, one time. Open it on **Mint**.
- **10 free daily letters:** one a day until you’ve had 10. The day resets at midnight UTC. A day you skip isn’t lost: you still get all 10, just later. Once you claim one, **Today** and **Mint** show the letter you got and how many are left.

To unlock them, prove you’re one person in one of three ways. Tap your picture in the game’s header to open [your profile](/profile) and link one:

- Link a **Farcaster** account. It counts for the wallets your Farcaster account has verified. To use it with another wallet, sign in with that wallet in your profile first.
- Link an **X** account that’s older than 90 days.
- Use a [Coinbase-verified](https://www.coinbase.com/onchain-verify) wallet.

Link as many as you like: it’s still one free pack and 10 daily letters for your whole profile. Everything else in the game works without any of them.

Free letters come from a shared pool of 30,000. They end when the pool runs out, or when about 95% of the dictionary has owners. Packs stay for sale either way.

## Packs

A **pack of five** lowercase letters costs **\$1 in \$WORD**. You can buy packs anytime, as many as you like. The letters pop out of the pack one at a time.

## Which letters you get

A letter’s odds mirror the dictionary. The 4,584 words have 22,920 letters between them, and each letter’s chance in a pack is its share of those slots. So E, S, A, R, and O come up often. The rarest letters are the **grails**: V, Z, X, J, and Q.

![The five grails, the rarest letters: V, Z, X, J, and Q](/images/grails.svg)

Every letter also has a fixed supply: 3 times its share of the dictionary, or **68,760 letters** in all. (The testnet game keeps its original supply, 57,294.)

## Your letters, onchain

Letters live onchain on Base, under the [ERC-1155](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-1155) standard for items you can hold several of. There are 52 letter IDs: 0 to 25 are lowercase a to z, and 26 to 51 are UPPERCASE A to Z.

- **They’re yours to move.** Send letters to another wallet, [swap](/showcase-and-swap#swap-letters) them with other players, or trade them anywhere on Base that supports ERC-1155.
- **The supply is fixed onchain.** The Letters contract enforces each letter’s cap. Once a letter’s supply has been minted, no more of it can be.
- **UPPERCASE comes only from rolls.** A successful [roll](/rolls) burns your lowercase letter and mints its UPPERCASE version. Only the game’s own contracts may do that, so an UPPERCASE letter can’t be minted any other way.

The contract addresses are on [contracts and standards](/contracts).

## Your bag

Your bag holds everything in your connected wallet, plus everything in the wallets your Farcaster account has verified, and in every wallet you’ve [linked in your profile](/profile#linked-wallets), shown together wherever you play.

The bag opens on your words, as [cards](/words#word-cards). Until you have a word, it opens on your letters. Tap **Letters** for your loose letters.

![The Bag’s letters in practice mode: lowercase and UPPERCASE letters, with a count on each letter you hold more than one of](/images/screen-bag.jpg)

Sort your letters **A to Z** or **most first**. On the Words side, filter your words (staked, not staked, earning, needs food, or for sale) and sort them (best first: rarest, then most raised, then most ascended; hungriest first; or A to Z). The bag remembers your choice in this browser.

Two details:

- New letters always arrive in the **wallet that’s connected**.
- Spelling, rolling, staking, and feeding need the **wallet that holds** the letter or word. To gather everything in one wallet, choose a [game wallet](/profile#your-game-wallet) and [move it](/profile#move-letters-between-wallets) there.

<Tip>
  If you play on the web with a wallet your Farcaster account hasn’t verified, verify it in Farcaster (Settings, then Verified addresses). Your whole bag then shows in both places.
</Tip>
