**\$WORD** is the token on Base behind Lexigotchi and [Let’s Have A Word!](/lets-have-a-word) Its contract is [`0x304e649e69979298bd1aee63e175adf07885fb4b`](https://basescan.org/token/0x304e649e69979298bd1aee63e175adf07885fb4b).

## What things cost

Prices are set in dollars and paid in \$WORD. As the price of \$WORD moves, the game updates the \$WORD amounts, so a pack costs about the same in dollars whatever \$WORD is worth. The game always shows the exact amount before you pay.

| Action | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Daily letter | Free |
| Pack of 5 letters | \$1.00 |
| Roll a letter | \$0.25 |
| Claim a word | From \$0.50, by rarity (below) |
| Snack | \$0.02 (your first each day is free) |

A word’s claim fee is the Common fee, \$0.50, times its [rarity’s](/words#rarity) weight, the same weight that sets its share of [earnings](/earning). Each tier’s fee stays the same multiple of the Common fee as \$WORD moves.

| Tier | Claim fee |
| --- | --- |
| Common | \$0.50 |
| Uncommon | \$1.00 |
| Rare | \$1.50 |
| Legendary | \$2.50 |
| Mythic | \$4.00 |

## Getting \$WORD

- **On the web**, swap right on the game’s **\$WORD** page: buy with ETH or USDC, or sell for either. It shows what you’ll get, the price impact, and the least you’ll accept before you confirm in your wallet.
- **Inside Farcaster**, the \$WORD page and **Buy \$WORD** (tap your balance in the header) open Farcaster’s own swap.

The footer of every screen shows the live \$WORD market cap and a link to the chart.

## The \$WORD page

The game’s **\$WORD** page (in the side rail, or **More** on a phone) shows the token’s live price and market cap, the total supply, how much has been burned, the pool’s liquidity, and how Lexigotchi and Let’s Have A Word! use it.

Burned \$WORD comes in two kinds, and the page shows both:

- **Burned (destroyed):** a burn destroys the tokens and lowers the total supply. Lexigotchi always burns this way.
- **At the dead address:** tokens sent to an address nobody controls. They can never move again, but the total supply still counts them.
