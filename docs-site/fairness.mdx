## The jackpot can’t be steered

The order of the daily secret words was locked onchain before the game launched, as a chain of hashes. Each day, the game reveals the next word, and anyone can check it against the chain. Nobody, not even the team, can change a future answer.

## A roll never costs you a letter

A missed roll never burns or downgrades your letter. It only raises your odds on the next roll. See [raising letters](/rolls).

## The rules are public

- The full dictionary, every word’s tier, and every letter’s odds come straight from the published word list.
- One word has exactly one owner, enforced by its NFT.
- The pools can only pay out what they hold.
- The code is open source on [GitHub](https://github.com/starl3xx/lexigotchi).
