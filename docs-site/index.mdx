Lexigotchi is a word-pet game on Base. Collect living letters, raise them, and spell words nobody else can own.

![Five letter characters spelling HEYOO and celebrating](/images/hero-heyoo.svg)

<Note>
  Lexigotchi runs on Base Sepolia, a test network, until its contracts launch on Base. Until then, the \$WORD in the game is test \$WORD with no value. Tap your balance and **Get test \$WORD** from the faucet. For the test [Shop](/shop), **Get test USDC** sits beside it (25 a day). If your wallet pays its own gas, it needs a little Base Sepolia ETH, which any Base Sepolia faucet gives out free.
</Note>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Collect letters">
    Free to start: a pack of 5, plus 10 free daily letters, for linking Farcaster, X, or a Coinbase-verified wallet. Or buy a pack of five anytime. Every letter is a little character, and every one arrives lowercase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Raise them">
    Roll a letter from lowercase to UPPERCASE. A miss never hurts it, and every miss raises your odds on the next roll.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spell words">
    Five letters make a word from the 4,584-word dictionary, in any mix of lowercase and UPPERCASE, and that word is yours alone: every word has exactly one owner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wake them every day">
    Stake your words and wake them every day. An awake word is in the running for the daily jackpot, and an all-UPPERCASE one earns \$WORD every day.
  </Step>
</Steps>
