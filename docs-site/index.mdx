Lexigotchi is a letter-collecting game on Base. Every letter is a little character you collect and raise. Spell a five-letter word from the dictionary and it’s yours alone. Keep your words fed, and they can win the daily jackpot and earn \$WORD.

![Five letter characters spelling HEYOO and celebrating](/images/hero-heyoo.svg)

<Note>
  Lexigotchi runs on Base Sepolia, a test network, until its contracts launch on Base. Until then, the \$WORD in the game is test \$WORD with no value. Tap your balance and **Get test \$WORD** from the faucet. If your wallet pays its own gas, it needs a little Base Sepolia ETH, which any Base Sepolia faucet gives out free.
</Note>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Collect letters">
    Get one free letter a day, or buy a pack of five anytime. Every letter arrives lowercase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Raise them">
    Roll a letter to raise it to UPPERCASE. A miss never hurts the letter, and every miss raises your odds on the next roll.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spell words">
    Use five letters of the same case to spell a word from the 4,584-word dictionary. Claim it and it’s yours alone: every word has exactly one owner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stake and feed them">
    Every staked word you keep fed is in the daily jackpot. A staked, fed word whose five letters are all UPPERCASE also earns \$WORD every day.
  </Step>
</Steps>
