## Staking

**Stake** a word to put it to work:

- Every word of yours that is staked and awake is in the running for the [daily jackpot](/jackpot). A sleepy word still counts; a word that is asleep sits out.
- A staked word whose five letters are all UPPERCASE also [earns \$WORD](/earning) every day.
- Matching staked words share the [weekly bounty](/bounty).

You can unstake a word anytime.

## Sleep

A staked word falls asleep if nobody wakes it.

| Mood | When | Daily jackpot | Earning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Awake | Woken within the last day | In | Full share |
| Sleepy | 1 to 2 days since it was last woken | In | Half share |
| Asleep | 3 or more days since it was last woken | Out | Nothing |

A sleeping word can’t win the jackpot or the bounty until you wake it.

![The word tease three ways: awake, sleepy, and asleep](/images/moods.svg)

## Waking

**Wake** a word, and it is awake again. Waking a word costs **\$0.02 in \$WORD**. The first word you wake each day is free: one a day for your whole profile, however many of your wallets stake words. What you pay to wake a word is burned. **Wake all**, on Today and in your Bag, wakes every word that needs it at once: yours, and the words you care for.

<Note>
  The sleep clock keeps running while a word is unstaked, so unstaking and restaking doesn’t reset it. Only waking the word does. A word you’ve just claimed starts awake.
</Note>

<Tip>
  The **Today** tab shows the card of the word that needs you most, on the [face](/showcase-and-swap#card-faces) you picked for it. Under the card, three marks say how the word stands: **Awake** (wide awake, sleepy, or asleep, and for how many days), **Jackpot** (in the running, or out while it’s asleep), and **Earns** (what it earns, or what would make it earn). Next to them is the one button for what it needs, such as **Wake**. A sleeping word’s card face fades toward bare paper until you wake it. Tap the arrows under the card, or swipe the card, to visit your other words. In your **Bag**, the same three marks are small icons beside each word’s name.
</Tip>

## Reminders

Turn on reminders and the game tells you:

- before a staked word falls asleep,
- when your free daily letter is ready,
- when you win the jackpot,
- when a word or letters of yours sell on the [Market](/showcase-and-swap#market),
- when your words win a share of the weekly [bounty](/bounty),
- if you turn it on, what your words [earned](/earning) each day they are paid, and
- if you turn it on, when the [Market](/showcase-and-swap#market) has the one letter that finishes a word nobody owns (once a day at most, and the same letter and word once a week at most).

A browser gets one reminder the moment you turn reminders on, so you can see that they work. The game waits for that reminder to go out: if your browser’s notification service refuses it, the game says so and leaves reminders off. Quit the browser fully and open it again, or use another browser.

If **Turn on reminders** fails, the game says why. Most often the browser couldn’t reach its own notification service: a VPN, a firewall, or a content blocker can stop it, and Brave needs **Use Google services for push messaging** turned on in its settings. The line under the message shows what the browser said, which helps if you report it.

### Choose which reminders you get

Open your profile (tap your picture). Under **What to tell me**, each kind of reminder has its own box: clear the ones you don’t want. Two start off, because they can come every day: the payday reminder and the letter alert. Tick them if you want them. Your choice covers email, your browser, Farcaster, and Telegram together, and every wallet linked to your profile.

How to turn them on:

- **By email:** add your email in [your profile](/profile#email-reminders) and type the 6-digit code we send you. Every reminder email has a one-click unsubscribe link. Too many? Under **What to tell me**, choose **One email a week**: one email with your week (what your words earned, what you sold, and which words need waking) in place of the emails that can come every day. A word about to fall asleep, a jackpot win, a sale, and a bounty share still come at once.
- **In Telegram:** tap **Link Telegram** in [your profile](/profile#telegram-reminders), then press **Start** in Telegram. The Lexigotchi bot sends your reminders as a message.
- **Inside Farcaster or the Base App:** add Lexigotchi and allow notifications.
- **On the web:** open your profile (tap your picture) or, on a phone, **More**, and tap **Turn on reminders**. On iPhone and iPad, first add Lexigotchi to your Home Screen (tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**), then turn reminders on from there.
