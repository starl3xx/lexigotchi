[Let’s Have A Word!](https://letshaveaword.fun) is a word hunt where everyone in the world chases the same secret five-letter word, and the finder wins the pot.

The two games share a lot:

- **The same dictionary.** Every word you can claim in Lexigotchi is a word you can guess there.
- **The same token.** Both run on [$WORD](/word).
- **Separate jackpots.** Each game draws its own secret word and pays its own pot.

The game’s **Let’s Have A Word!** page shows the live round, the last round, and how to play.
