title: "Earning $WORD"
description: "All-UPPERCASE words that are staked and fed earn $WORD every day."
---

Every day, **1% of the Rewards Pool** is shared among the words that are:

- staked,
- fed (not hungry), and
- all UPPERCASE.

Each word’s share is weighted by its [tier](/words#rarity): a Legendary word takes eight times a Common word’s share. A peckish word earns half its share, and a hungry word earns nothing.

Because each day pays a fraction of the pool rather than a fixed amount, the pool never runs dry.

## Collecting your rewards

Rewards add up day by day. When you have some to collect, the **Today** tab says so, with a button to collect them. One tap collects everything waiting across your bag, including any [bounty](/bounty) you’ve won.

## Where the pool comes from

Part of every pack, roll, and claim fee goes to the Rewards Pool. See [where fees go](/fees).
