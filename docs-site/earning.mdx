title: "Earning $WORD"
description: "All-UPPERCASE words that are staked and awake earn $WORD every day, paid automatically."
---

Every day, **1% of the Rewards Pool** is shared among the words that are:

- staked,
- awake (not asleep), and
- all UPPERCASE.

Each word’s share is weighted by its [tier](/words#rarity): a Mythic word takes eight times a Common word’s share. A sleepy word earns half its share, and a word that is asleep earns nothing. Each [ascension](/ascension) stage multiplies a word’s share by 1.1, up to 1.61 times at Holographic.

Because each day pays a fraction of the pool rather than a fixed amount, the pool never runs dry. The **Today** tab shows how much the Rewards Pool holds now. Tap it to see what your words have earned.

## Getting paid

There’s nothing to claim. Every day at 00:05 UTC, the game works out each earning word’s share and pays it **straight to the wallet that holds the word**. You don’t sign anything or pay gas, and nothing expires. A [bounty](/bounty) you win is paid the same way.

The game tells you what landed: a note when you open it after a payout, and a line under your words on the **Today** tab.

## Seeing what your words earned

Tap that line, or the earnings row on a fully raised word, to open **Your earnings**. On a word with letters still to raise, the row opens the roll for its next letter instead.

![Your earnings in practice mode: everything your words have earned and what today’s payout brought, then each word with what it has earned and what would make it earn: VIVID is sleepy and earns half, the others have letters to raise](/images/screen-earnings.jpg)

It shows:

- everything your words have earned, and what the latest payout brought,
- each word’s total, and what it earned at the latest payout, and
- for each word that isn’t earning a full share, what would change that: wake it, stake it, or raise its last letters.

In your **Bag**, an earning word shows a coin with what it earned at the latest payout.

### Share your earnings

Once your words have earned something, **Your earnings** has a **Share** button. It makes a picture: your top word’s card, wearing its gilding if it has ascended, and beside it a tag with everything your words have earned and what the latest payout brought. Post it on X, cast it on Farcaster, or tap **Save image** to keep it.

The amounts in the picture are the game’s own, worked out when you open the sheet, so a picture always shows what your words really earned. It shows \$WORD only, never dollars, and no name or wallet. On the test game it says **test \$WORD**.

If a payout is ever late, what it owes you waits for the next one. The earnings sheet shows it, with a button to collect it yourself if you’d rather not wait.

## Where the pool comes from

Part of every pack, roll, and claim fee goes to the Rewards Pool. See [where fees go](/fees).
