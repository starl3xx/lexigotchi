---
title: "The daily jackpot"
description: "One secret word wins every day."
---

Every day, the game reveals one **secret word**. If a player owns that word, and it’s staked and not hungry at the draw, they win the whole pot. The case of the word doesn’t matter.

If nobody holds the word, staked and fed, the pot **rolls over** and grows for the next day.

## Your tickets

Every staked word of yours that isn’t hungry is a ticket. Because every word has one owner, there’s never more than one winner.

## When it draws

The draw happens just after **midnight UTC**. The game shows a countdown.

## Where the pot comes from

**10%** of every pack, roll, and claim goes into the jackpot. The team doesn’t add to it. See [where fees go](/fees).

## Nobody can steer it

The order of secret words was locked onchain before the game launched, as a chain of hashes. Each day’s word is revealed from that chain, so nobody, not even the team, can change a future answer. See [fairness](/fairness).
