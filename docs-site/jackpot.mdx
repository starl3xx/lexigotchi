Every day, the game reveals one **secret word**. If you hold it, staked and fed, the whole pot is yours. The case of the word doesn’t matter.

![The word lucky celebrating: the owner of today’s secret word takes the whole pot](/images/jackpot.svg)

If nobody holds the word, staked and fed, the pot **rolls over** and grows for the next day.

## Your words in the running

Every word you keep staked and fed is in the running. “Fed” means not hungry when the word is revealed: a peckish word still counts, and only a hungry one sits out. Because every word has one owner, there’s never more than one winner.

The **Jackpot** tab shows the pot, the countdown, and your words in the running, with this week’s [bounty](/bounty) next to them. The **Today** tab shows the pot too, beside the bounty and the [Rewards Pool](/earning).

## When the word is revealed

Each day’s word is revealed just after **midnight UTC**. The game shows a countdown.

## Where the pot comes from

**10%** of every pack, roll, claim, and ascension attempt goes into the jackpot. The team doesn’t add to it. See [where fees go](/fees).

## Nobody can steer it

The order of secret words was locked onchain before the game launched, as a chain of hashes. Each day’s word is revealed from that chain, so nobody, not even the team, can change a future answer. The locked order never repeats a word, so a word that has been drawn won’t come up again. See [fairness](/fairness).
