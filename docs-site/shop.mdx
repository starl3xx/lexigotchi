The Shop sells things for your words’ [cards](/words#word-cards). Open **Shop** in the side menu on a computer, or tap **More**, then **Shop**, on a phone. You can look around before you connect a wallet.

<Note>
  Lexigotchi runs on Base Sepolia, a test network, until its contracts launch on Base. Until then, the Shop takes test USDC, which is free and has no value: tap your balance, then **Get test USDC** (25 a day).
</Note>

## What’s in it

The Shop has two parts, behind the switch at the top:

- **Card faces.** A [card face](/showcase-and-swap#card-faces) is the picture on the front of a card, behind the word. Buy a face once, then pick it for any of your words: tap the palette button under the stage on **Today**, or **Face** under a word’s card when you open it. The first face in the Shop is **Outer Space**: a small world under the stars, where your letters stand on the curve of a planet.
- **Accessories.** Things a card can carry besides its face. None are in the Shop yet.

Each thing shows what it is, what it costs, and whether you already have it. A card face is bought once: when you have it, the Shop says **You have it** and doesn’t sell it to you again.

## Limited editions

Some things are sold as a limited edition: only so many are made. A limited edition says so on its card in the Shop, with how many were made and how many are left. When none are left, it says **Sold out**. Everyone who has one keeps it.

## Paying

Everything in the Shop is paid for in USDC on Base. Your wallet approves exactly what the thing costs, and it shows as yours as soon as the purchase lands.

Each purchase is split in the same transaction: 80% goes to the studio, and 20% buys \$WORD for the [Rewards Pool](/fees), which pays staked words.

## What you buy stays with you

What you buy in the Shop belongs to you, not to a word. Nothing you do with a word takes it away:

- **Sell a word**, and its new owner gets it on the stock face. You keep the face for your other words.
- **Dissolve a word**, and the face is still yours.
- **Stake a word, or move it** to another wallet in your [profile](/profile), and it keeps the face you picked.

Nothing from the Shop can be sold, sent, or traded. It stays with your profile, across the wallets you’ve linked to it.
