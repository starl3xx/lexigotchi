Your wallet is your Lexigotchi account. Your profile adds the accounts that unlock your [free letters](/letters#free-letters) (a pack of 5 plus 10 daily letters) and give you a name and picture. Tap your picture in the game’s header to open it. To leave this wallet and connect another, tap **Disconnect** at the top.

![Your profile with a wallet connected and nothing linked yet: the Disconnect button, the free letters, the three accounts you can link, the locked name and picture, and email reminders](/images/screen-profile.jpg)

## Linked accounts

| Account | Free letters | Name and picture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Farcaster | Yes | Yes |
| X | Yes, once the X account is older than 90 days | Yes |
| Coinbase-verified wallet | Yes | No |

One of them unlocks the free letters. Link more if you like: you still get **one free pack and 10 daily letters**, not more for each account.

### Farcaster

Inside Farcaster, your Farcaster account is linked for you. On the web, tap **Link** next to Farcaster and sign in with Farcaster.

### X

Tap **Link** next to X. Your wallet asks you to sign a message first, which is free and sends nothing onchain. Then X asks you to approve Lexigotchi. Lexigotchi reads your X handle, name, picture, and the date you joined X, one time. It never posts for you, and it keeps no access to your X account.

X’s approval screen says the app can see the posts and accounts you can view. X asks for that permission before any app can read your profile. Lexigotchi reads only your own profile, once, and then lets go of the access.

- **Your X account is public.** Anyone can see which X account is linked to your wallet, and the leaderboard and the dictionary can show it for you.
- **The free letters need an X account older than 90 days.** A newer account still links and gives you a name and picture. Your profile shows the date its free letters unlock.
- **One X account goes with one wallet.** Link it from a second wallet and it moves there.
- **You can unlink or move it anytime**, except on a day the X account took the free letter. Then it stays where it is until midnight UTC, so one day can’t give two letters.

Inside Farcaster, X opens in your browser. When X is done, go back to the game.

### Coinbase verification

[Verify your wallet with Coinbase](https://www.coinbase.com/onchain-verify), then come back and tap **Check again**. A Coinbase-verified wallet unlocks the free letters. It doesn’t give you a name or picture.

## Linked wallets

Play with more than one wallet? Link them, up to five, and they become one account: one bag, one profile (name, picture, X, and email), and one set of free letters between them.

1. Tap **Link another wallet**. Your wallet signs you in first, which is free and sends nothing onchain.
2. Sign with the other wallet. On the same device, switch to it in your wallet app (or with **Switch wallet**), and the card asks it to sign. On another device, or inside Farcaster, tap **Copy link** and open the link there with that wallet connected.
3. That wallet signs a message naming your account. It’s free and sends nothing onchain. The link works once, for 15 minutes.

Good to know:

- **Letters and words stay where they are.** Your bag shows every linked wallet’s letters and words, but you spell, roll, stake, and wake words with the wallet you’re connected with, using what that wallet holds. To gather them in one wallet, [move them](#move-letters-between-wallets).
- **Linked wallets are public.** The leaderboard counts them as one player, under your profile’s name and picture.
- **The whole account gets one set of free letters (a pack of 5 plus 10 daily letters), and the first word it wakes each day is free.** Linking never adds to either.
- **Reminders cover the whole account.** The warning that a word is about to fall asleep comes for words staked by any of your wallets, by email, in Telegram, in Farcaster, or in the browser where you turned reminders on. A “your daily letter is ready” reminder by email, Telegram, or the browser skips any day one of your wallets already took it. A sale by any of your wallets is told to the account once. **What to tell me**, in your profile, chooses which reminders the account gets.
- **A wallet with its own X link or email** can’t join until you remove those in that wallet’s profile.
- **Wallets your Farcaster account verified join on their own.** Sign in with Farcaster, or with a wallet Farcaster verified, and its other verified wallets join your profile, marked **Via Farcaster**. They count toward the five. To take one out, remove its verification in Farcaster; it leaves the next time you open the game. A Farcaster wallet already in another profile, or with its own X or email, stays where it is.
- **Unlink anytime** (wallets you linked yourself) from the Wallets card, except on a day your account took its free letter or woke a word for free. Then the wallets stay together until midnight UTC. The account’s first wallet can’t leave; unlink the others instead.

### Your game wallet

Your letters, words, and \$WORD are onchain, in whichever wallet holds them. When you spell, roll, stake, or wake a word, the game uses the connected wallet’s own letters, words, and \$WORD. So if you play with more than one wallet, choose one **game wallet** and keep everything there.

- **Choose it** in the Wallets card: tap **Make game wallet** next to the wallet you play with most. You can change it anytime.
- **Inside Farcaster**, you can only use your Farcaster wallet. If you play mostly in Farcaster, make it your game wallet.
- **The game tells you when something is in the wrong place.** If the wallet you’re using isn’t your game wallet and holds letters or words, it offers to move them there. If your things are in your game wallet and you’re connected with another, it says to switch. Tap **×** to hide the message on Today and in your Bag. It comes back only when it has something new to tell you, such as another wallet holding letters. The Wallets card in your profile always shows what each wallet holds.
- **Stake from your game wallet.** Only the wallet that staked a word can wake it, unless you name your other wallets as caretakers (below).

### Caretakers: wake words from any of your wallets

In the Wallets card, under **Care for staked words from any wallet**, the wallet you’re connected with can name your other wallets as caretakers. A caretaker can wake, roll, and ascend the words that wallet stakes, so you can wake them wherever you play: inside Farcaster or on the web.

- Each wallet names its own caretakers, so do it once from each wallet that stakes words.
- A caretaker pays from its own wallet. It can’t unstake a word or take one: only the staking wallet unstakes.
- The first word woken each day is free, and it is counted for the staking wallet’s profile, whoever wakes the word. Your profile gets one a day, whichever wallet wakes the word and whichever wallet staked it.
- Tap **Stop** to take a caretaker away anytime.

### Move letters between wallets

Each wallet in the Wallets card shows what it holds. To move everything from one wallet to another:

1. Connect the wallet that holds the letters. Only that wallet can send them.
2. Tap **Move this wallet’s letters**, check the wallet it goes to, and tap **Move**.
3. Your wallet sends one transaction. With Coinbase Wallet the move is free: the game pays the network fee. Other wallets need a little ETH on Base.

What moves: every loose letter, lowercase and UPPERCASE, and every word the wallet holds. Tick **Also move my \$WORD** to send your \$WORD too: it pays for spelling, rolling, and waking your words, so the wallet you play with needs it. Staked words stay staked in their wallet; unstake one first to move it. A move waits while a roll on one of the letters is still open. Letters the wallet has [for sale](/showcase-and-swap#sell-letters) pause once they leave it, and a word’s listing stops. The game can’t undo a move, and it only sends to a wallet already in your account.

## Name and picture

Your name and picture come only from a linked Farcaster or X account. Until you link one, the game shows a short version of your wallet address.

If you link both, you choose which one names you. Choosing asks you to sign in with your wallet, which is free and sends nothing onchain. If you don’t choose, Farcaster names you.

Your name and picture show on the leaderboard and next to the words you own in the dictionary.

Your picture also shows as a small stamp on the back of your [word cards](/words#word-cards), beside the card’s number, and on the back in a video you make of a card. To leave it off, clear **Show my picture on my cards** in your profile. That choice is kept on the device you make it on, so make it on each device you play on.

## Email reminders

Add an email and the game tells you before a staked word falls asleep, when your free daily letter is ready, when something of yours sells on the Market, and when your words win the jackpot or a share of the weekly bounty. It’s optional. Under **What to tell me** you choose which reminders you get, and how often email comes: each one as it happens, or [one email a week](/care#reminders).

1. Tap **Add email**. Your wallet asks you to sign a message, which is free and sends nothing onchain.
2. Enter your email and tap **Send code**.
3. Type the 6-digit code from the email and tap **Confirm**. The code works for 10 minutes.

Your email is never shown to anyone, and one email goes with one wallet. To stop reminders, clear **Email me reminders** in your profile, or use the unsubscribe link in any reminder email. **Remove** deletes the email from your profile.

## Telegram reminders

Link Telegram and the Lexigotchi bot sends you the same reminders as a message: the ones you keep on under **What to tell me**. It’s optional, and you can have it with email or without.

1. Tap **Link Telegram**. Your wallet may ask you to sign a message, which is free and sends nothing onchain.
2. Tap **Open Telegram**. It opens a chat with the Lexigotchi bot.
3. Press **Start** in that chat. The bot says you’re linked.

The link from step 2 works once, for 15 minutes. If it has expired, tap **Link Telegram** again.

Your Telegram account is never shown to anyone. One Telegram account goes with one profile: linking it to another profile moves it. To stop the reminders, tap **Unlink** in your profile, or send the bot `/unlink`.
