Your wallet is your Lexigotchi account. Your profile adds the accounts that unlock the [free daily letter](/letters#the-daily-letter) and give you a name and picture. Tap your picture in the game’s header to open it.

![Your profile with a wallet connected and nothing linked yet: the free daily letter, the three accounts you can link, the locked name and picture, and email reminders](/images/screen-profile.jpg)

## Linked accounts

| Account | Free daily letter | Name and picture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Farcaster | Yes | Yes |
| X | Yes, once the X account is older than 90 days | Yes |
| Coinbase-verified wallet | Yes | No |

One of them unlocks the free daily letter. Link more if you like: you still get **one letter a day**, not one for each account.

### Farcaster

Inside Farcaster, your Farcaster account is linked for you. On the web, tap **Link** next to Farcaster and sign in with Farcaster.

### X

Tap **Link** next to X. Your wallet asks you to sign a message first, which is free and sends nothing onchain. Then X asks you to approve Lexigotchi. Lexigotchi reads your X handle, name, picture, and the date you joined X, one time. It never posts for you, and it keeps no access to your X account.

- **Your X account is public.** Anyone can see which X account is linked to your wallet, and the leaderboard and the dictionary can show it for you.
- **The daily letter needs an X account older than 90 days.** A newer account still links and gives you a name and picture. Your profile shows the date its daily letter unlocks.
- **One X account goes with one wallet.** Link it from a second wallet and it moves there.
- **You can unlink or move it anytime**, except on a day the X account took the free letter. Then it stays where it is until midnight UTC, so one day can’t give two letters.

Inside Farcaster, X opens in your browser. When X is done, go back to the game.

### Coinbase verification

[Verify your wallet with Coinbase](https://www.coinbase.com/onchain-verify), then come back and tap **Check again**. A Coinbase-verified wallet unlocks the free daily letter. It doesn’t give you a name or picture.

## Name and picture

Your name and picture come only from a linked Farcaster or X account. Until you link one, the game shows a short version of your wallet address.

If you link both, you choose which one names you. Choosing asks you to sign in with your wallet, which is free and sends nothing onchain. If you don’t choose, Farcaster names you.

Your name and picture show on the leaderboard and next to the words you own in the dictionary.

## Email reminders

Add an email and the game tells you before a staked word goes hungry, when your free daily letter is ready, and if you win the jackpot. It’s optional.

1. Tap **Add email**. Your wallet asks you to sign a message, which is free and sends nothing onchain.
2. Enter your email and tap **Send code**.
3. Type the 6-digit code from the email and tap **Confirm**. The code works for 10 minutes.

Your email is never shown to anyone, and one email goes with one wallet. To stop reminders, clear **Email me reminders** in your profile, or use the unsubscribe link in any reminder email. **Remove** deletes the email from your profile.
